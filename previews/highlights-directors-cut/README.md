# Highlights: Director's Cut (look-only preview)

A clickable preview of a portfolio "highlight" for PhilFreela profiles. It is **not part of the website**: nothing in `client/` loads it, and nothing is saved anywhere.

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge, pick one of the four sample freelancers, and press **Play with sound**.

## What it shows

- **Card draw opening.** Tap the card to open it. The freelancer's level decides the card:

  | Level | Card | Opening |
  |---|---|---|
  | Beginner | Plain, no rarity word | Simply turns over to show the profile |
  | Average | Blue, "Rare" | Spins open |
  | Intermediate | Purple, "Epic" | Cracks, then about 5 more seconds of suspense |
  | Advanced | Gold, "Legendary" | About 10 seconds, then "You found a better person." |

- **No timer.** The viewer moves through the parts by tapping, pressing Next, using the arrow keys or swiping. Each change plays a 5-second chapter card for the next part.
- **Projects open one by one.** They show the freelancer's @name mark and can't be downloaded.
- **Live music.** It is made in the page and gets stronger with the level.

The level is a score out of 100. Premium does not count.

| Part of the score | Points |
|---|---|
| Projects done | 35 |
| Ratings received | 20 |
| Share of 4-5★ ratings | 25 |
| Portfolio | 10 |
| Services | 5 |
| Verified ID | 5 |

Beginner is under 40, Average 40-64, Intermediate 65-84, and Advanced 85 and up.

Sample data only: the four freelancers are made up, the profile pictures are drawn, the photos are stock samples, and the short video clips were made from those photos.
